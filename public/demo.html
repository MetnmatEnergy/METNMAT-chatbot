Metnmat Chat Widget

This page loads the same embed script you will add to metnmat.com. Click the blue chat button in the bottom-right corner to test product search and support.

Add to your website (before </body>)

<script src="CHATBOT_URL/widget.js" data-site-key="metnmat-main"></script>
Replace CHATBOT_URL with your deployed server URL (HTTPS). After deploy, open /integrate for a copy-ready snippet.
